Dubai


Dubai is the most populous city in the United Arab Emirates and the capital of the Emirate of Dubai.[9] It is on a creek on the southeastern coast of the Persian Gulf. As of 2025, its population stands at 4 million,[6] 92% of whom are expatriates.[10] The wider urban area includes Sharjah and has a population of 5 million people as of 2023,[7] while the Dubai–Sharjah–Ajman metropolitan area has a population of 6 million people.
